Know about me

Who am I?

I’m Andrew Nandolo, a freelance graphic designer and web developer based in Zomba, but working wherever work needs me to. I am an individual who is efficient, organised, hardworking, well-disciplined, focused, self-motivated and dependable. I am also a team player, creative and logical thinker and a fast learner who is able to adapt and promote designing and development standards and principles which are constantly advancing.

My approach is collaborative, focused, and built around real business and organisational outcomes—without sacrificing the spark that makes people pay attention.
